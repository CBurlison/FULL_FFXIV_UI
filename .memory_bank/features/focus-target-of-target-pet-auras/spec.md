# Focus, Target-of-Target, and Pet Auras Specification

## Scope
- Implement and maintain the feature as defined in this specification.
- Ensure seamless compatibility across WoW Retail and WoW Forever.
- Maintain combat lockdown safety, automated testing, and UI styling standards.

## Functional Requirements

### General Requirements
- Implement and maintain the feature as defined in this specification.
- Ensure seamless compatibility across WoW Retail and WoW Forever.
- Maintain combat lockdown safety, automated testing, and UI styling standards.
- Focus and target-of-target frames also display compact resource-colored bars beneath their health bars.
- These move and scale with their respective frame anchors, refresh on unit and resource changes, and hide when the unit is absent or its resource maximum is zero.
- The pet frame shows up to eight buffs and eight debuffs in separate 22-pixel icon rows to its right, with stack counts, duration timers, and hover tooltips.
- Both rows move, scale, and hide with the pet frame.
