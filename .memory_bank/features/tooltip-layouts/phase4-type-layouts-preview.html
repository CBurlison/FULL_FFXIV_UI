0. Scope and status

Proposed Phase 4 layouts for all eleven groups in one page. Nothing here is implemented. Values are illustrative, not taken from dumps. Each group needs your approval before Lua/XML changes, and Retail acceptance per group follows implementation.

XML static surfaces and FontString pools (trait cells, stat pairs, text rows, icons).
Lua data, values, colours, which sections show, native capture and restore.
hidden sections not used by a type are created hidden and never shown for that type.

1. Equipment (first to implement)

Defense and Damage/DPS/Speed metric cells sit under the header. Item level is a full-width band. Requirements follow, then a two-column stat grid with beneficial (green) and detrimental (red) colours. A comparison block shows deltas for the equipped item. Effects have a heading, divider and text. Durability and Sell Price sit at the footer on one left edge.
